Alentra Sensor Logic
  • Continuously monitors simulated pH readings during the installation sequence.
  • Uses the expected gastric pH range of 1.5–3.5.
  • When the simulated reading enters the gastric range, Alentra reports Sensor Installed.
  • Selecting Deflate starts a simulated balloon pressure decrease.
  • A sustained pressure drop produces the Installation Complete signal.

  report("Sensor installed");
